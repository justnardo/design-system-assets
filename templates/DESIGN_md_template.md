## Worked example: Viceroy Elderly Care Services

Industry: Elder Care
Tone: Warm, professional, trustworthy, editorial

## Color Palette

- Primary (blush-rose): #805158
- Secondary (sage-green): #4f634f
- Background (warm-ivory): #fbfaee
- Text (deep-taupe): #1b1c15

## Typography

- Headline: Noto Serif
- Body: Manrope

## Style Directives

The site should feel editorial on story pages and use clean grids on action pages.

What we use:
- Editorial layouts on story pages
- Soft shadow under cards
- Warm natural lighting in photos
- Clean grid systems

What we never use:
- No glassmorphism
- No gradient buttons
- No parallax effects
- Do not use harsh contrast
